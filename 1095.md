# Show 1095: Westside Cowboy, Opinions on untitled (halo) & RIP Richard Lloyd of Television
![westside](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/hi-res-image-westside-cowboy-by-clementine-schneidermann-3.jpeg)
Hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot talk with members of Westside Cowboy, a rising indie rock band from Manchester, England. The hosts also review the new album from young indie rockers Untitled (Halo) and pay tribute to guitarist Richard Lloyd.

## 00:00:00 Review: untitled (halo)
![halo](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/untitled-halo-untitled-halo-album-art.webp)
{artist : Untitled (Halo)} are a mysterious band from Los Angeles with a self-titled debut album ({album : untitled (halo)}) that they describe as “California melancholia.” Jim is a fan of the album and likens the obliqueness to early shoegaze bands. Greg is disappointed in the lack of strong songs. 


## 00:00:00 Feature: Obit: Richard Lloyd

{artist : Richard Lloyd} was a founding guitarist of {artist : Television} who went on to work extensively with {artist : Matthew Sweet}. He died November 7 at age 75.

## 00:00:00 Interview: Westside Cowboy
![westside](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/hi-res-image-westside-cowboy-by-clementine-schneidermann-3.jpeg)
{artist : Westside Cowboy} came of age during the COVID era and have now developed into one of the most exciting bands going. Their debut full length, {album : It Goes On}, is one of hosts Jim and Greg’s favorite albums of 2026. Band members Reuben Haycocks and Aoife Anson O'Connell join the hosts to talk about how they’re distinguishing between studio recording and live sound and how the idea of {artist : Jeff Tweedy} helps them write lyrics.
