# Show 1083: Courtney Barnett, Opinions on Phoebe Bridgers & Twisted Teens
![Phoebe Bridgers Lost Weekend](https://static.soundopinions.org/assets/1083/012.jpg)

After Australian singer-songwriter Courtney Barnett released her 2015 album Sometimes I Sit and Think and Sometimes I Just Sit, she quickly became a top tier indie rock act. All of her skills were on display when Courtney Barnett joined hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot for an interview and intimate solo performance in front of an audience. Plus, the hosts review the new album from Phoebe Bridgers and Twisted Teens.

## 00:00:00 Review: Phoebe Bridgers

![Phoebe Bridgers Lost Weekend](https://static.soundopinions.org/assets/1083/012.jpg)

{artist : Phoebe Bridgers}' third solo album, “{album : Lost Weekend}” is one of the most anticipated of the year. With a long list of producers and collaborators the album could be overstuffed, but somehow it's brilliant.



## 00:00:00 Review: Twisted Teens

{artist : Twisted Teens} appeared on host Greg Kot's midyear “Best Albums” list. Now they're back with another album- and reportedly working on a third. “{album : Florida Water Blues}” is a similarly brilliant effort, with a higher quotient of studio experimentation.

## 00:00:00 Interview: Courtney Barnett

{artist : Courtney Barnett} is a mainstay in the indie rock world now, but in 2016 she was promoting her debut album when she did a Sound Opinions session at the Goose Island Tap Room in Chicago. She talks about her beginnings in Australian coffeehouses and performs solo versions of her songs on electric guitar.
