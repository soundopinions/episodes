# Show 1078: Chinese Hip Hop
![wall](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/9780593491850.jpeg)
This week, hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot talk with author Yi-Ling Liu about Chinese hip hop and how the music breaks through the infamous "fire wall" to connect to listeners. They'll highlight some key artists and tracks, as well as put the music into the larger context.
