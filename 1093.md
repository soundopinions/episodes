# Show 1093: Stevie Wonder's "Songs in the Key of Life" 50th Anniversary



## 00:00:00 Intro: Coming Soon

It's been 50 years since Stevie Wonder released his masterpiece "Songs in the Key of Life." This week, hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot revisit their dissection of the double album and EP, plus they discuss four newly released tracks and whether "Songs in the Key of Life" is Stevie Wonder's best album. They'll also hear from some listeners.
