# Show 1087: Phoebe Bridgers Plus Opinions on Westside Cowboy
![phoebe](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/screenshot-2026-09-17-at-11-25-40-am.png)
In 2026, Phoebe Bridgers is one of the biggest artists in the world. This week, hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot revisit their 2018 interview and live performance with Phoebe, which includes never-before-aired content. They discuss her debut album, songwriting prowess and impact on music. They'll also review the debut album from Westside Cowboy.
