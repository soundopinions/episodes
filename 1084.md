# Show 1084: Melissa Auf der Maur (Hole, The Smashing Pumpkins) & Opinions on Steve Lacy

![melissa](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/819gdml9owl-sl1500.jpg)

This week, hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot talk with musician and author Melissa Auf der Maur. In 1994, Melissa was suddenly thrust into the spotlight when she joined Hole as a bassist for the “Live Through This” world tour. They talk about Melissa's memoir, Even the Good Girls Will Cry, and about Courtney Love, Billy Corgan and the power of music. Jim and Greg also review the new album from Steve Lacy.
