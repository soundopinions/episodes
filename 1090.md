# Show 1090: Fall Review Roundup: Opinions on Neil Young, This is Lorelei, Michael Kiwanuka & More

![fall](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/untitled-design.png)

Hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot review some new albums from Michael Kiwanuka, Neil Young, Chaka Khan, The Tubs and This is Lorelei. They also bid farewell to Cheetah Chrome, guitarist for the Dead Boys and Rocket from the Tombs, and hear from some listeners.
