## 00:00:00 Feature: Ranking Rock Music
![rock](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/bill-wyman-rock-lists-book.jpg)

As an early co-host of Sound Opinions, {artist : Bill Wyman} has a deep familiarity with hosts Jim DeRogatis and Greg Kot. This allows them to waste no time getting into heated arguments over Wyman’s rankings of songs by The Beatles, Nirvana, Michael Jackson and more.
