## 00:00:00 Feature: Buddy Guy
![buddy](https://static.soundopinions.org/images/2026/buddy-guy-jv04.jpg)
Hosts Jim and Greg celebrate blues legend {artist : Buddy Guy}’s 90th birthday. They begin by outlining his unconventional career, including his star turn in 2025’s {tag : Sinners}. He’s known for his violent, high-energy style. This style wasn’t appreciated by his former label {label : Chess} Records, but was adored and emulated by British blues fans like Eric Clapton, Jeff Beck and Andy Summers. Then they revisit their 2007 interview and Buddy also performs a rare acoustic set. Finally, the hosts share some of their favorite tracks from Guy’s career and trace his influence on younger musicians.
